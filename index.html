01About

I'm an MSc student in Quantum Science & Engineering at EPFL, currently writing my thesis in Singapore at the Department of Mathematics, National University of Singapore with Prof. Ying Chen, on optimal transport and generative modelling — and how they connect to quantum machine learning.

Before that I spent six months at Alice & Bob in Paris, building a GPU-accelerated, differentiable steady-state solver for cat-qubit simulations that is now part of Dynamiqs. My work sits where learning meets hard physics: quantum error correction decoding, scientific ML and surrogate models, and quantitative finance.

On the side, I ship products — an AI résumé tool and a marketplace for dental students.

ML / Optimization team

03Publication
arXiv:2608.30860 · quant-ph · 2026

Well-conditioned iterative methods for large open quantum systems

Gaspard Beugnot, Paul Gregory, Rémi Robin, Antoine Tilloy

The no-jump part of the Lindblad evolution can be inverted cheaply. We use it to build a well-conditioned auxiliary map, giving an iterative method at O(n³) per iteration for steady states and low-lying modes — competitive on both CPU and GPU.

ML for error correction

Quantum computers make errors all the time, and software has to correct them fast. I trained a model that spots the rare hard cases and sends only those to a slow, exact corrector — about 100× fewer errors at almost no extra cost.
